# Data Story — Cuisine Revenue Performance

## Performance Summary

Three cuisines exceeded their revenue targets: North Indian, South Indian, and Italian.

North Indian generated ₹216,297 against a target of ₹180,000, which is ₹36,297 above target.

South Indian generated ₹63,417 against a target of ₹50,000, which is ₹13,417 above target.

Italian generated ₹14,735 against a target of ₹10,000, which is ₹4,735 above target.

Three cuisines were below target. Chinese generated ₹127,840 against a target of ₹140,000, a shortfall of ₹12,160.

Fast Food generated ₹55,123 against a target of ₹60,000, a shortfall of ₹4,877.

Desserts generated ₹19,694 against a target of ₹25,000, a shortfall of ₹5,306.

## Recommendations

1. Prioritize Chinese for targeted improvement because it has the largest revenue shortfall of ₹12,160.

2. Study the performance of North Indian and South Indian, which exceeded their targets by ₹36,297 and ₹13,417 respectively, and apply useful approaches from these stronger-performing cuisines to the below-target categories.
